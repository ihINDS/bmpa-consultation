# BMPA Stakeholder Consultation

Guide to the Barbados Medical Products Authority stakeholder consultation on the draft Medical Products Regulations and Clinical Trial Regulations, held on Tuesday 20 October 2026 at the PAHO/WHO Office for Barbados and the Eastern Caribbean Countries.

The site is a single self-contained page (`index.html`) served with GitHub Pages. It includes:

- the workshop agenda
- duties by type of organisation under the Act and the drafts
- a searchable digest of both draft Regulations
- a deadline calculator
- WHO maturity levels and how other regulators fit with the BMPA
- the draft medicine classification lists
- a registration route finder and process walk-throughs
- points for comment and a comment builder
- contact through WhatsApp and email

Written comments: BMPRA@barbados.gov.bb

This is a plain-language guide. The Barbados Medical Products Act, 2026 and the final Regulations govern.
